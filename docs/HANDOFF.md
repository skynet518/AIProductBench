## CURRENT RELEASE

**AIProductBench CN V1 is PUBLISHED.**

AIProductBench CN V1 is a practical model-selection benchmark for Chinese LLMs,
built for AI product teams choosing models under real quality, cost, and latency
constraints.

The benchmark evaluates model-selection trade-offs rather than attempting to
produce a universal “best model” ranking.

Primary decision dimensions:

- Quality
- Cost
- Latency
- Constraint adherence
- Workload fit
- Pareto efficiency
